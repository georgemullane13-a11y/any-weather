Our approach

Fewer shortcuts, fewer callbacks

Roofing has a reputation problem, and it is not hard to see why. A roof is the one part of a house most people never see, which makes it the easiest place to cut a corner — a cement fillet instead of lead, a tile laid over a rotten batten, a flat roof with no fall in it.

We built this company to work the other way round. Every job starts with a proper diagnosis, including a look in the loft where it matters, and every quote sets out what we are actually doing in writing before anyone commits to anything.

That means we occasionally talk people out of work they were expecting to pay for. It also means most of our work comes from people who have used us before, or who were told about us by someone who did.

Local, and staying local

We are based in Plymouth and work in Plymouth. There is no call centre, no national franchise and no doorstep sales operation — you speak to the people who will be on your roof, and we are still down the road if anything needs looking at again.

Being local also shapes the work itself. Roofs here deal with salt-laden air off the Sound and Atlantic south-westerlies that drive rain sideways into details that would stay dry inland. We specify fixings and flashing details with that in mind, because a roof that would be fine in the Midlands is not necessarily fine in Plymstock.
