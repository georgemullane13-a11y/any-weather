Why homeowners choose us

A roof is only as good as the layers you cannot see

Anyone can lay tiles. What decides whether a roof still keeps water out in twenty years is the membrane, the battens, the fixings and the flashing detail underneath — the parts nobody inspects from the pavement.

  • We diagnose before we quoteIncluding a look in the loft, because the leak is rarely where the stain is.
  • Specified for the South WestSalt air and Atlantic south-westerlies punish fixings and details. We allow for that.
