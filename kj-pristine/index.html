About KJ Pristine

I understand the struggle, because I live it too

Messy rooms, endless laundry and stuff everywhere. Some days it feels never-ending. I know, because that bedroom photo is my daughter’s room.

KJ Pristine started as an idea: a busy mum with big dreams. There was a business plan, insurance, a DBS check, supplies, a uniform and flyers, and then one day I decided to actually do it. Now it’s real homes, real clients and real bookings.

I’m Kerri, and I built KJ Pristine as a premium, owner-operated cleaning and housekeeping service for busy families across South Manchester and Cheshire. I’m Level 5 British Cleaning qualified, and when you book, you get me. No rotating strangers, no rushed jobs. Just a careful, consistent clean tailored to your home and lifestyle.

Kerri Jackson, Owner

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. KJ Pristine is fully insured, DBS checked and Level 5 British Cleaning qualified, so you can feel completely comfortable having me in your home.

How much does a clean cost?

Every home is different, so I offer a free walk-through and a bespoke quotation. Decluttering & organisation starts from £35 per hour and can be added to a clean or booked on its own.

Which areas do you cover?

All areas of South Manchester and Cheshire, including Wilmslow, Alderley Edge, Prestbury, Hale, Bowdon, Sale, Wythenshawe, Gatley, Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Stockport, Bramhall and Hazel Grove. If you’re nearby, just ask.

How long does a clean take?

Most regular cleans are booked as 2 or 3 hour visits. Deep cleans and home resets take longer depending on the size of your home, and you’ll get a clear estimate at your free walk-through.

Can you help before or after guests, parties or Airbnb stays?

Yes. Whether it’s getting guest-ready, a post-party reset or an Airbnb turnover, just get in touch and I’ll tailor a quote.

Are you pet friendly?

Yes. Pets are very welcome, and I use eco-conscious products that are kinder to your home and everyone in it.

What products do you use?

Eco-friendly products that are kinder to your home, your family and your pets.

How does payment work?

Regular cleans are paid on the day of your clean. Deep cleans and home resets are secured with a 25% deposit, with the balance due on completion. Cash and bank transfer are both accepted.

What if I’m not happy with something?

Your satisfaction means everything. Let me know within 24 hours and I’ll do my best to put it right.

My house is really messy. Is that okay?

Absolutely. No judgement, no stress. Real life gets messy (my own daughter’s room is proof!). I’m here to give practical help getting things back under control.
