Not sure what you need?

Tell us the symptom — we will find the cause

Most people call us about a damp patch, not a failed flashing. That is fine. Describe what you can see and we will work out what is actually causing it, then tell you what it will take to put right.

  • Damp on an upstairs ceilingUsually a slipped slate, a failed flashing or a blocked valley — rarely where the stain appears.
  • Water running down an outside wallTypically guttering: blocked, sagging, or set to the wrong fall.
  • Damp on the chimney breastNearly always the stack itself — pointing, flaunching or flashing.
  • Puddles on a flat roofFalls in the wrong place, and a sign the covering is on borrowed time.
